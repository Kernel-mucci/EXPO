Your team isn't the problem. They care deeply. But when every shift feels like crisis management — when SOPs don't match reality, training is all tribal knowledge, and your POS network is wide open — it's a bandwidth problem, not a people problem.

What We Build

EXPO embeds in your operation and builds the systems your team needs but your leadership doesn't have time to create.

We document what's in people's heads before it walks out the door. We design workflows that survive Saturday night. We build the digital tools — ordering systems, prep dashboards, checklists, reporting — that turn manual chaos into repeatable process.

And we make sure your technology actually works together instead of creating more problems.
