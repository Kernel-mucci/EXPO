About EXPO

Where culinary instinct meets systems thinking

Our Philosophy

The Pass Is Everything

In any kitchen, the expo station is where it all comes together. It's the last checkpoint before dishes go out. Where tickets get organized, orders get fired, and quality gets confirmed. When the pass is dialed in, service flows. When it's not, everything backs up.

That's how we think about operations.

EXPO is the checkpoint for your business. We're where chaos becomes clarity. Where scattered processes become coordinated systems. Where fire-drill management becomes strategic operations.

We operate at the intersection of:

  • Culinary Precision — The discipline of mise en place, the logic of flow, the importance of consistency
  • Systems Architecture — Frameworks that scale, processes that repeat, documentation that lives
  • Technical Security — Protecting digital assets, hardening networks, training teams on threats
  • Operational Efficiency — Eliminating waste, streamlining workflows, optimizing tools

Where fire meets firewall. Where hospitality meets hardening. That's EXPO.

Who's Behind This

EXPO was built by operators who got tired of watching good teams struggle with bad systems.

Five years in food & beverage — on the line, behind the bar, managing shifts. We saw the same problems everywhere: SOPs that nobody followed, training that lived in one person's head, POS systems wide open to attack, and managers drowning in tasks that should have been systematized months ago.

So we built EXPO. Part operations consultancy, part systems architecture, part cybersecurity practice — all focused on the unique challenges of hospitality. Based in Missoula. Building for the Northwest and beyond.
