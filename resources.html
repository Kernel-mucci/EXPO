Frequently Asked Questions

About EXPO

What makes EXPO different from other hospitality consultants?

We're not consultants who've never worked a Saturday rush. EXPO was founded by a team with five years of hands-on F&B experience who got tired of seeing the same operational problems repeat. We combine battle-tested kitchen experience with technical expertise in areas like cybersecurity and systems architecture. We build solutions that survive reality, not just theory.

Where is EXPO based and who do you serve?

We're based in Missoula, Montana, and primarily serve the Northwest (Montana, Idaho, Washington, Oregon). However, many of our services—especially systems documentation, security audits, and training development—can be delivered remotely to clients anywhere.

Do you work with single-unit operations or only multi-site brands?

We work with both, though our sweet spot is 2-10 location groups. Single-unit operators preparing to scale can absolutely benefit from getting systems right before opening location two. Large established groups often need help tightening systems that have drifted over time.

Services & Pricing

How much does EXPO cost?

We price based on deliverables and outcomes, not hourly rates. A focused project (like building an onboarding program or conducting a security audit) typically ranges from $3,000-$15,000 depending on scope and complexity. Retainer engagements and fractional operations partnerships are quoted based on time commitment and strategic depth. Every project starts with a free consultation where we'll give you a clear proposal.

What's the typical timeline for a project?

Most project-based engagements take 2-8 weeks from kickoff to final deliverable. Discovery and audit phases typically take 1-2 weeks. Solution development and implementation depend on complexity. We move fast because we know time is your most valuable resource.

Can I hire EXPO for just one specific thing?

Absolutely. While our four pillars work best in integration, we're happy to tackle single, well-defined projects. Need just a security audit? A service playbook? Documentation for one specific process? We can do that.

Do you offer ongoing support or just one-time projects?

Both. We offer project-based work for specific deliverables, monthly retainers for ongoing optimization and strategic guidance, and fractional operations partnerships for deeper, longer-term engagements. You choose what fits your needs.

Working Together

What does the engagement process look like?

It starts with a free consultation where we discuss your challenges and determine if we're a good fit. If we move forward, we begin with discovery/audit, then move to solution development, implementation support, and ongoing optimization (if desired). Every step includes clear deliverables and regular communication.

How involved does my team need to be?

We need access to your operations for discovery (observations, interviews, document review) and collaboration on solution design to ensure what we build actually fits your culture and reality. Implementation requires your team's engagement for training and adoption. We minimize disruption but can't build solutions in a vacuum.

What if the solution you build doesn't work for us?

We build iteratively and include feedback loops throughout the process. If something isn't landing right, we adjust. Our goal is to build solutions your team will actually use, which means flexibility and responsiveness are baked into our approach.
